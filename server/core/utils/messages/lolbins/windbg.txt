ABOUT
    Windows Debugger for advanced user-mode and kernel-mode debugging.
    LOLBAS documents executing an executable under a trusted
    Microsoft-signed binary.

USAGE:
    windbg.exe -g {CMD}

EXAMPLES:
    windbg.exe -g {CMD}

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    windbg.exe -g {CMD}
