ABOUT
    Control Panel item launcher.
    Loads DLLs from ADS paths or executes .cpl (DLL) applets for AWL
    bypass.

USAGE:
    control.exe {PATH_ABSOLUTE}:evil.dll
    control.exe {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.cpl}

EXAMPLES:
    control.exe {PATH_ABSOLUTE}:evil.dll
    control.exe {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.cpl}

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    control.exe {PATH_ABSOLUTE}:evil.dll
