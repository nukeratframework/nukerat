ABOUT
    Microsoft Copilot app. LOLBAS documents executing a process under a
    trusted Microsoft-signed binary.

USAGE:
    mscopilot.exe --no-startup-window --disable-gpu-sandbox --gpu-launcher="{CMD} && taskkill /f /im mscopilot.exe &&"

EXAMPLES:
    mscopilot.exe --no-startup-window --disable-gpu-sandbox --gpu-launcher="{CMD} && taskkill /f /im mscopilot.exe &&"

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    mscopilot.exe --no-startup-window --disable-gpu-sandbox --gpu-launcher="{CMD} && taskkill /f /im mscopilot.exe &&"
