ABOUT
    Symbolic Debugger for Windows. LOLBAS documents executing an
    executable under a trusted Microsoft-signed binary.

USAGE:
    ntsd.exe -g {CMD}

EXAMPLES:
    ntsd.exe -g {CMD}

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    ntsd.exe -g {CMD}
