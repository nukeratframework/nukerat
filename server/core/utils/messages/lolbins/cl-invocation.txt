ABOUT
    Aero diagnostics PowerShell helper. Dot-source the script, then call
    SyncInvoke to launch a program (proxy execution).

USAGE:
    . C:\Windows\diagnostics\system\AERO\CL_Invocation.ps1
    SyncInvoke {CMD}

EXAMPLES:
    . C:\Windows\diagnostics\system\AERO\CL_Invocation.ps1
    SyncInvoke {CMD}

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    . C:\Windows\diagnostics\system\AERO\CL_Invocation.ps1
    SyncInvoke {CMD}
