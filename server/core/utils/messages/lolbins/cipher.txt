ABOUT
    EFS cipher maintenance tool.
    Secure-delete with /w or encrypt files with /e to wipe evidence or
    tamper with defenses.

USAGE:
    cipher /w:{PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder}
    cipher.exe /e {PATH_ABSOLUTE}

ARGUMENTS:
    /w:                          Wipe free space / deleted content in folder
    /e                           Encrypt files at path

EXAMPLES:
    cipher /w:{PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder}
    cipher.exe /e {PATH_ABSOLUTE}

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    cipher /w:{PATH_ABSOLUTE:folder}
