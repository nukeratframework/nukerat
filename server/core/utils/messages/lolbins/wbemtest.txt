ABOUT

    commands through WMI classes.

USAGE:
    wbemtest.exe

EXAMPLES:
    wbemtest.exe

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    wbemtest.exe
