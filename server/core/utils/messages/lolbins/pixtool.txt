ABOUT
    Command line utility for taking and analyzing PIX GPU captures.
    LOLBAS documents executing an executable under a trusted,
    Microsoft-signed binary.

USAGE:
    pixtool.exe launch {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.exe}

EXAMPLES:
    pixtool.exe launch {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.exe}

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    pixtool.exe launch {PATH_ABSOLUTE:.exe}
