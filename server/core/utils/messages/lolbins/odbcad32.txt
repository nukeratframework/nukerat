ABOUT
    ODBC Data Source Administrator to manage User/System DSNs and
    ODBC drivers. LOLBAS documents execute a binary as a high-
    integrity process without a UAC prompt.

USAGE:
    odbcad32.exe

EXAMPLES:
    odbcad32.exe

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    odbcad32.exe
