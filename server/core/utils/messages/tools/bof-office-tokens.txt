ABOUT
    Collects Office JWT access tokens from memory of the specified Office process.

USAGE:
    bof-office-tokens --- <pid>

ARGUMENTS:

    pid    Target Office process ID

EXAMPLES:

    bof-office-tokens --- 4321

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-office-tokens -w 60 --- 4321
