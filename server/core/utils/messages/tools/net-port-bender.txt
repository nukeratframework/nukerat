ABOUT
    Redirect a local port to a remote host through the beacon.

USAGE:
    net-port-bender --- <arguments>

EXAMPLES:
    net-port-bender --- --help

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    net-port-bender -w 60 --- <arguments>
