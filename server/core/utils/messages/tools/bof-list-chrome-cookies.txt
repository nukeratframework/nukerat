ABOUT
    Lists targetable browser subprocesses for cookie dumping. Use the
    returned PID with bof-cookie-katz. x64 only.
USAGE:
    bof-list-chrome-cookies --- </chrome|/edge|/webview>

ARGUMENTS:
    /chrome|/edge|/webview Browser to enumerate (leading slash required)

EXAMPLES:
    bof-list-chrome-cookies --- /chrome
    bof-list-chrome-cookies --- /edge
    bof-list-chrome-cookies --- /webview

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-list-chrome-cookies -w 60 --- /chrome
