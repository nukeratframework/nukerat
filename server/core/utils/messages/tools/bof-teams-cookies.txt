ABOUT
    Steals Microsoft Teams cookies by running inside the ms-teams.exe process.
    Extracts encryption key and filelessly downloads the Cookies database.

USAGE:
    bof-teams-cookies
EXAMPLES:
    bof-teams-cookies
NOTES:
    Must be executed within the ms-teams.exe process context. Takes no arguments.
    Use the repo decrypt.py script to extract secrets from the downloaded database.
