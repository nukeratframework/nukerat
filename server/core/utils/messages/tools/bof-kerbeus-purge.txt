ABOUT
    Purge Kerberos tickets from the current or specified logon session.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-purge --- [/luid:LOGONID]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-purge
NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-purge -w 60 --- [/luid:LOGONID]
