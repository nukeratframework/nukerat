ABOUT
    .NET tool that dumps LSASS to a minidump and runs a customized in-memory Mimikatz to
    extract logonpasswords and ekeys from the dump, then deletes the dump file.

USAGE:
    net-safety-katz
EXAMPLES:
    net-safety-katz
NOTES:
    Requires SeDebugPrivilege. Dumps to C:\Windows\Temp\debug.bin before Mimikatz execution.
