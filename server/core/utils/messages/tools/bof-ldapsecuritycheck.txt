ABOUT
    Check LDAP signing and channel binding requirements on a domain controller.

USAGE:
    bof-ldapsecuritycheck --- [DC]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-ldapsecuritycheck    bof-ldapsecuritycheck --- dc01.corp.local

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-ldapsecuritycheck -w 60 --- dc01.corp.local
