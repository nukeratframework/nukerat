ABOUT
    List running processes including PID, PPID, and ComandLine (uses wmi)

USAGE:
    bof-tasklist --- [opt:server]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-tasklist
NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-tasklist -w 60 --- [opt:server]
