ABOUT
    Renew a TGT.
USAGE:
    bof-renew --- /ticket:BASE64 [/dc:DC] [/ptt]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-renew --- /ticket:BASE64 /ptt

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-renew -w 60 --- /ticket:BASE64 /ptt
