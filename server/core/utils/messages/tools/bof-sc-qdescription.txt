ABOUT
    Sc qdescription implementation in BOF
USAGE:
    bof-sc-qdescription --- <service name> [opt:server]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-sc-qdescription --- Spooler

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-sc-qdescription -w 60 --- Spooler
