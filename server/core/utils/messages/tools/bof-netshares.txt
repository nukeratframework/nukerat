ABOUT
    List shares on the local or remote computer

USAGE:
    bof-netshares --- [hostname]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-netshares    bof-netshares --- dc01

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-netshares -w 60 --- dc01
