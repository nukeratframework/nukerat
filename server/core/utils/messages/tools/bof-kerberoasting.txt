ABOUT
    Lists SPN enabled user or service accounts or requests service tickets
    (TGS REP) for offline cracking with Hashcat. Output tickets appear
    between TICKET tags. Convert with TicketToHashcat.py.
USAGE:
    bof-kerberoasting --- <list|list-no-aes|roast|roast-no-aes> [account]

ARGUMENTS:
    list                List SPN enabled accounts
    list-no-aes         List SPN enabled accounts without AES encryption
    roast               Request tickets for all matching accounts
    roast-no-aes        Request tickets without AES encryption
    account             Optional sAMAccountName filter (default: all)

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerberoasting --- list
    bof-kerberoasting --- list-no-aes
    bof-kerberoasting --- roast
    bof-kerberoasting --- roast-no-aes
    bof-kerberoasting --- roast svc-test

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerberoasting -w 60 --- list
