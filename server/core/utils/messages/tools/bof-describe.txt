ABOUT
    Parse and describe a Kerberos ticket.
USAGE:
    bof-describe --- /ticket:BASE64

EXAMPLES:
    bof-describe --- /ticket:BASE64

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-describe -w 60 --- /ticket:BASE64
