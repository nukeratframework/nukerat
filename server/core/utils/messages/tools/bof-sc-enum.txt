ABOUT
    Enumerate services for qc, query, qfailure, and qtriggers info

USAGE:
    bof-sc-enum --- [opt:server]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-sc-enum
NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-sc-enum -w 60 --- [opt:server]
