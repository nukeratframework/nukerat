ABOUT
    Finds Chrome or Edge NetworkService processes, duplicates an open handle
    to the Cookies SQLite database, and saves a copy to
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\<pid>.db. Scans chrome.exe and msedge.exe processes
    automatically.
USAGE:
    bof-handle-stealer
EXAMPLES:
    bof-handle-stealer
NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-handle-stealer -w 60
    bof-handle-stealer -bg
