ABOUT
    Requests service tickets and outputs crackable TGS hashes in hashcat
    format.

USAGE:
    net-sharp-roast --- <target> [domain\\user] [password]

TARGET:
    all                           Roast all users in current domain
    <username>                    Roast specific user
    <SPN>                         Roast specific service principal name
    <OU DN>                       Roast users in specified OU
    LDAP://DC=domain,DC=local     Roast users in trusted domain
    <SPN>@domain                  Roast SPN in trusted domain

EXAMPLES:
    net-sharp-roast --- all
    net-sharp-roast --- sqlservice
    net-sharp-roast --- "MSSQLSvc/SQL.corp.local"
    net-sharp-roast --- harmj0y "corp.local\\user" "Password123!"

NOTES:
    Deprecated. Functionality incorporated into net-rubeus kerberoast action.
