ABOUT
    Request AS REP for users without Kerberos preauthentication.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-asreproasting --- /user:USER [/dc:DC] [/domain:DOMAIN]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-asreproasting --- /user:svc_no_preauth /domain:corp.local

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-asreproasting -w 60 --- /user:svc_no_preauth /domain:corp.local
