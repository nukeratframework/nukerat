ABOUT
    Get target server or domain's configured password policy and lockouts

USAGE:
    bof-get-password-policy --- <hostname>

EXAMPLES:
    bof-get-password-policy --- dc01.corp.local

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-get-password-policy -w 60 --- dc01.corp.local
