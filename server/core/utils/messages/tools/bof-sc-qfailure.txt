ABOUT
    Query a service for failure conditions

USAGE:
    bof-sc-qfailure --- <service name> [opt:server]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-sc-qfailure --- Spooler

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-sc-qfailure -w 60 --- Spooler
