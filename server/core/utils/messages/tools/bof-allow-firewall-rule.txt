ABOUT
    Add inbound or outbound firewall allow rule.

USAGE:
    bof-allow-firewall-rule --- <in|out> <port> <rule_name> <group_name> <description>

EXAMPLES:
    bof-allow-firewall-rule --- out 8080 MyRule MyGroup Allowing outbound traffic

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-allow-firewall-rule -w 60 --- out 8080 MyRule MyGroup Allowing outbound traffic
