ABOUT
    Add a local or remote user.

USAGE:
    bof-adduser --- <user> <password> <hostname>

EXAMPLES:
    bof-adduser --- newuser P@ss123 localhost

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-adduser -w 60 --- newuser P@ss123 localhost
