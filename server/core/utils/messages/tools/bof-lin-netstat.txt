ABOUT
    (linux) Show network connections from /proc/net.

USAGE:
    bof-lin-netstat --- [flags]

ARGUMENTS:
    flags               Optional [-tlnpu]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-lin-netstat
    bof-lin-netstat --- -tlnp
NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-lin-netstat -w 60 --- [flags]
