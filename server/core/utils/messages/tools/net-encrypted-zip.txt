ABOUT
    Compresses a file/directory and encrypts the ZIP with AES256 CFB. Clears key from memory with RtlZeroMemory.

USAGE:
    net-encrypted-zip --- encrypt <path> <key> | decrypt <path> <key>

EXAMPLES:
    net-encrypted-zip --- encrypt C:\data mykey
    net-encrypted-zip --- decrypt C:\out.zip.enc mykey
