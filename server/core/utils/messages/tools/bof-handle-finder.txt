ABOUT
    List processes that have open handles to a file.

USAGE:
    bof-handle-finder --- <path\to\file>

EXAMPLES:
    bof-handle-finder --- C:\Windows\System32\ntdll.dll
    bof-handle-finder --- C:\Windows\explorer.exe

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-handle-finder -w 60 --- <arguments>
