ABOUT
    Enumerates system processes and reports protection level, service
    relation, user, session ID, and file path. Useful for identifying
    unprotected third party service processes that may be candidates for DLL
    hijacking.
USAGE:
    bof-enumprotections
EXAMPLES:
    bof-enumprotections
NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-enumprotections -w 60
    bof-enumprotections -bg
