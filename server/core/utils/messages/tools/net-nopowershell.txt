ABOUT
    PowerShell-like command execution in .NET without System.Management.Automation.dll.
    Runs in-memory via execute-assembly, avoiding PowerShell logging. Supports pipelines,
    AD cmdlets, WMI, registry, networking, and archive operations.

USAGE:
    net-nopowershell --- <command> [arguments] [| <command> ...]

EXAMPLES:
    net-nopowershell --- Get-Process
    net-nopowershell --- Get-ADUser -Filter * | Select-Object Name,SamAccountName
    net-nopowershell --- Get-WmiObject Win32_OperatingSystem
    net-nopowershell --- Get-ChildItem C:\Users
    net-nopowershell --- Test-NetConnection dc01.corp.local -Port 445

NOTES:
    Pipeline characters must be surrounded by spaces. See CHEATSHEET.md for full cmdlet list.
    TLS 1.1+ not supported (.NET Framework 2).
