ABOUT
    List members of a group. Supports standard LDAP (389) with
    signing/sealing and LDAPS (636).
USAGE:
    bof-get-groupmembers --- {group} [-ou {path}] [-dc {ip}]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-get-groupmembers --- {group} [-ou {path}] [-dc {ip}]

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-get-groupmembers -w 60 --- {group} [-ou {path}] [-dc {ip}]
