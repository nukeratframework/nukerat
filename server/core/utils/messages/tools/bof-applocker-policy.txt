ABOUT
    Enumerates AppLocker policy configurations, rule collections, and enforcement modes by
    scanning the relevant registry keys.

USAGE:
    bof-applocker-policy

EXAMPLES:
    bof-applocker-policy

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-applocker-policy -w 60
    bof-applocker-policy -bg
