ABOUT
    Stop a running scheduled task.

USAGE:
    bof-schtasks-stop --- <hostname> <folder/taskname>

EXAMPLES:
    bof-schtasks-stop --- dc.corp.local \MyFolder\MyTask

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-schtasks-stop -w 60 --- dc.corp.local \MyFolder\MyTask
