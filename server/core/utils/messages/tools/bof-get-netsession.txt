ABOUT
    Enumerate sessions on the local or specified computer

USAGE:
    bof-get-netsession --- [opt:computer]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-get-netsession
NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-get-netsession -w 60 --- [opt:computer]
