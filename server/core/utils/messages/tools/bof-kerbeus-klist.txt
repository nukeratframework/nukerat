ABOUT
    List Kerberos tickets with detailed information.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-klist --- [/luid:LOGINID] [/user:USER] [/service:SERVICE] [/client:CLIENT]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-klist --- /service:krbtgt

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-klist -w 60 --- /service:krbtgt
