ABOUT
    Query a service for trigger conditions

USAGE:
    bof-sc-qtriggerinfo --- <service name> [opt:server]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-sc-qtriggerinfo --- Spooler

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-sc-qtriggerinfo -w 60 --- Spooler
