ABOUT
    Dumps LSASS credentials by reading physical memory through signed vulnerable kernel
    drivers with MmMapIoSpace primitives. Supports MSV1 (logonpasswords) and WDigest
    credential extraction. Requires administrator privileges. May cause a BSOD if a driver
    fails during operation.

USAGE:
    bof-ghostkatz --- <mode> [-prv <provider id>]

MODES:
    logonpasswords    Dump MSV1 logon session credentials
    wdigest           Dump WDigest credentials

PROVIDERS:
    1    Toshiba TPwSav
    2    TechPowerUp ThrottleStop

EXAMPLES:
    bof-ghostkatz --- logonpasswords -prv 1
    bof-ghostkatz --- wdigest -prv 2
    bof-ghostkatz --- wdigest

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-ghostkatz -w 60 --- logonpasswords -prv 1
