ABOUT
    Identifies Attack Surface Reduction (ASR) rules, actions, and exclusion locations by
    querying MSFT_MpPreference via WMI. Requires an elevated context.

USAGE:
    bof-asrenum

EXAMPLES:
    bof-asrenum

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-asrenum -w 60
    bof-asrenum -bg
