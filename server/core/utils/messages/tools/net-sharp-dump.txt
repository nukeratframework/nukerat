ABOUT
    C# port of Out-Minidump.ps1. Creates a compressed minidump of a process (LSASS
    by default) using MiniDumpWriteDump, gzip-compresses it, and deletes the raw dump.

USAGE:
    net-sharp-dump --- [pid]

ARGUMENTS:
    pid    Target process ID (default: LSASS)

EXAMPLES:
    net-sharp-dump    net-sharp-dump --- 8700

NOTES:
    Output saved to C:\Windows\Temp\debug<PID>.bin (gzip). Rename to .gz to decompress.
    Use sekurlsa::minidump and sekurlsa::logonPasswords on same OS/arch with Mimikatz.
