ABOUT
    Delete a registry key or value.

USAGE:
    bof-reg-del --- <hostname> <Hive> <Path> [ValueName]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-reg-del --- localhost HKCU "Software\Test" MyVal

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-reg-del -w 60 --- localhost HKCU "Software\Test" MyVal
