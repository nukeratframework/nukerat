ABOUT
    Read first bytes of exported functions from a loaded DLL.

USAGE:
    bof-mem-read --- <dll> <function1> [function2 ...]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-mem-read --- ntdll.dll NtCreateFile NtOpenProcess

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-mem-read -w 60 --- ntdll.dll NtCreateFile NtOpenProcess
