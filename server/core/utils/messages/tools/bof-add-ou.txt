ABOUT
    Create organizational unit. Supports standard LDAP (389) with
    signing/sealing and LDAPS (636).
USAGE:
    bof-add-ou --- {name} [-parent {path}] [-dc {ip}] [--ldaps]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-add-ou --- {name} [-parent {path}] [-dc {ip}] [--ldaps]

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-add-ou -w 60 --- {name} [-parent {path}] [-dc {ip}] [--ldaps]
