ABOUT
    Reads the msDS-ManagedPassword blob from a Group Managed Service Account (gMSA) via LDAP
    and parses the values into NTLM hashes for reuse.

USAGE:
    net-gmsa-password-reader --- --AccountName <account> [options]

OPTIONS:
    --AccountName         Required. sAMAccountName of the gMSA
    --DomainName          Domain the account belongs to
    --DomainController    Hostname of a domain controller to query
    --LdapPort            LDAP port (default: 389)

EXAMPLES:
    net-gmsa-password-reader --- --AccountName jkohler
    net-gmsa-password-reader --- --AccountName arobbins --DomainName testlab.local
