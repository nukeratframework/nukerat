ABOUT
    Remove ACE from DACL. Supports standard LDAP (389) with signing/sealing
    and LDAPS (636).
USAGE:
    bof-remove-ace --- {target} [-trustee {trustee}] [-index {idx}] [options]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-remove-ace --- {target} [-trustee {trustee}] [-index {idx}] [options]

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-remove-ace -w 60 --- {target} [-trustee {trustee}] [-index {idx}] [options]
