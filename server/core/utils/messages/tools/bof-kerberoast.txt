ABOUT
    Requests a Kerberos service ticket (AP REQ) for a given Service
    Principal Name and outputs a base64 encoded ticket for offline cracking.
    Convert output with apreq2hashcat.py.
USAGE:
    bof-kerberoast --- <target_spn>

ARGUMENTS:
    target_spn          Service Principal Name to kerberoast

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerberoast --- <target_spn>

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerberoast -w 60 --- <target_spn>
