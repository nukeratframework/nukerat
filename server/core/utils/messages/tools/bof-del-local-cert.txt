ABOUT
    Delete a local computer certificate from a specified store based on its unique thumbprint.

USAGE:
    bof-del-local-cert --- <store> <thumbprint>

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-del-local-cert -w 60 --- <store> <thumbprint>
