ABOUT
    Request a service ticket from a TGT.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-asktgs --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:SPN1,SPN2,... [/domain:DOMAIN] [/dc:DC] [/tgs:BASE64] [/targetdomain:DOMAIN] [/targetuser:USER] [/enctype:{rc4|aes256}] [/ptt] [/keylist] [/u2u] [/opsec]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-asktgs --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:cifs/dc.corp.local /ptt

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-asktgs -w 60 --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:cifs/dc.corp.local /ptt
