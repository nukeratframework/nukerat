ABOUT
    Find a specific loaded module in all processes or list all loaded modules in a specific process.

USAGE:
    bof-enum-lib --- <option> <target> [pid]

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-enum-lib -w 60 --- <option> <target> [pid]
