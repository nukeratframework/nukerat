ABOUT
    Extract saved Wi-Fi passwords from the local system.

USAGE:
    bof-wi-fi-passwords --- (no arguments)

EXAMPLES:
    bof-wi-fi-passwords

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-wi-fi-passwords -w 60 --- (no arguments)
