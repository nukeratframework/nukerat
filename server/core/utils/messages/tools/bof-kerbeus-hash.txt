ABOUT
    Generate Kerberos key hashes from a password.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-hash --- /password:PASSWORD [/user:USER] [/domain:DOMAIN]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-hash --- /password:P@ss /user:admin /domain:corp.local

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-hash -w 60 --- /password:P@ss /user:admin /domain:corp.local
