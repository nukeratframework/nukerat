ABOUT
    Check if a specific TCP port is open on a local or remote host.

USAGE:
    bof-probe --- <host> <port> [timeout_seconds]

ARGUMENTS:

    host              Target IP or hostname (local or remote)

    port              TCP port number

    timeout_seconds   Connect timeout in seconds (default: 5)

EXAMPLES:
    bof-probe --- 127.0.0.1 445
    bof-probe --- 192.168.15.10 9090
    bof-probe --- 10.10.10.5 445 10

OUTPUT:
    host:port OPEN     Port is reachable
    host:port FAILED   Port is closed or unreachable (not a BOF error)

NOTES:
    Works against remote hosts, not just localhost.
    Remote probes need a reasonable timeout (default 5s). Without timeout the
    BOF may report FAILED on reachable ports.

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-probe -w 60 --- 192.168.15.10 9090 10
