ABOUT
    List domain groups or group members from the default or specified domain.

USAGE:
    bof-netgroup --- [opt:domain]
        bof-netgroup --- <groupname> [opt:domain]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-netgroup    bof-netgroup --- "Domain Admins" corp.local

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-netgroup -w 60 --- "Domain Admins" corp.local
