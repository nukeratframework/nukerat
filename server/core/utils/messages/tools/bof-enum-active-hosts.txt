ABOUT
    Enumerate active hosts or verify a single open port. Tool accepts list with predefined hostnames or IP addresses and is loaded from your own operator client.

USAGE:
    bof-enum-active-hosts --- <hostfile> <port> <timeout>

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-enum-active-hosts -w 60 --- <hostfile> <port> <timeout>
