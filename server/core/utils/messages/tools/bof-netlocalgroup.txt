ABOUT
    List local groups or local group members on the local or specified computer.

USAGE:
    bof-netlocalgroup --- [opt:server]
        bof-netlocalgroup --- <groupname> [opt:server]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-netlocalgroup    bof-netlocalgroup --- Administrators

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-netlocalgroup -w 60 --- Administrators
