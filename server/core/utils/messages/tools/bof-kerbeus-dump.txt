ABOUT
    Extract Kerberos tickets from logon sessions.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-dump --- [/luid:LOGINID] [/user:USER] [/service:SERVICE] [/client:CLIENT]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-dump --- /user:administrator

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-dump -w 60 --- /user:administrator
