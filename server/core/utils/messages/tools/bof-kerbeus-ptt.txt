ABOUT
    Submit a ticket to the current or specified logon session.
USAGE:
    bof-kerbeus-ptt --- /ticket:BASE64 [/luid:LOGONID]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-kerbeus-ptt --- /ticket:BASE64

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-kerbeus-ptt -w 60 --- /ticket:BASE64
