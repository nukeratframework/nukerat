ABOUT
    Patch, check, and revert AMSI and ETW for x64 processes. Compares the
    first 10+ bytes of AmsiScanBuffer and EtwEventWrite. Does not load
    amsi.dll if it is not already present in the current process. x64 only.
USAGE:
    bof-patchit --- <command_id>

COMMANDS:
    1    check       Check if AMSI and ETW are patched
    2    all         Patch both AMSI and ETW
    3    amsi        Patch AMSI (AmsiScanBuffer)
    4    etw         Patch ETW (EtwEventWrite)
    5    revertAll   Revert patched AMSI and ETW
    6    revertAmsi  Revert patched AMSI
    7    revertEtw   Revert patched ETW

EXAMPLES:
    bof-patchit --- 1
    bof-patchit --- 2
    bof-patchit --- 3
    bof-patchit --- 4
    bof-patchit --- 5
    bof-patchit --- 6
    bof-patchit --- 7

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-patchit -w 60 --- 1
