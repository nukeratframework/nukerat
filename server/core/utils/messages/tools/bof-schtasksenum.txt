ABOUT
    Enumerate scheduled tasks on the local or remote computer

USAGE:
    bof-schtasksenum --- [opt:server]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-schtasksenum
NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-schtasksenum -w 60 --- [opt:server]
