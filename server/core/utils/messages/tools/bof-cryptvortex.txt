ABOUT
    Encrypt or decrypt files in a directory.

USAGE:
    bof-cryptvortex --- encrypt <password> "<directory>" <encrypted_ext> "<ext1,ext2,...>"
    bof-cryptvortex --- decrypt <password> "<directory>" <encrypted_ext>

EXAMPLES:
    bof-cryptvortex --- encrypt secret@123 "C:\data\" .enc ".txt,.ps1"

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-cryptvortex -w 60 --- encrypt secret@123 "C:\data\" .enc ".txt,.ps1"
