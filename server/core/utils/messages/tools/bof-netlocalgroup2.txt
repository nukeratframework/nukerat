ABOUT
    Modified version of `netLocalGroupListMembers` that supports BOFHound

USAGE:
    bof-netlocalgroup2 --- [opt:groupname] [opt:server]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-netlocalgroup2    bof-netlocalgroup2 --- Administrators

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-netlocalgroup2 -w 60 --- Administrators
