ABOUT
    List open handles in a process.

USAGE:
    bof-list-process-handles --- <pid>

EXAMPLES:
    bof-list-process-handles --- 1234

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-list-process-handles -w 60 --- 1234
