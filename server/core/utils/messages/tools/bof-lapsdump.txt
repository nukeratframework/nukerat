ABOUT
    Dumps LAPS passwords from specified computers within Active Directory.
    Requires elevated AD privileges.
USAGE:
    bof-lapsdump --- <Computername>

ARGUMENTS:
    Computername        Target IP or hostname

EXAMPLES:
    bof-lapsdump --- DC01
    bof-lapsdump --- 192.168.1.10

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-lapsdump -w 60 --- DC01
