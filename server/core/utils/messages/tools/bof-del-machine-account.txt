ABOUT
    Delete a rogue machine account created in Active Directory.

USAGE:
    bof-del-machine-account --- <Computername>

EXAMPLES:
    bof-del-machine-account --- DCPWN$

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-del-machine-account -w 60 --- <arguments>
