ABOUT
    Perform S4U2self and S4U2proxy constrained delegation abuse.
USAGE:
    bof-s4u --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:SPN {/impersonateuser:USER | /tgs:BASE64} [/domain:DOMAIN] [/dc:DC] [/altservice:SERVICE] [/ptt] [/nopac] [/opsec] [/self]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-s4u --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:cifs/target /impersonateuser:admin /ptt

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-s4u -w 60 --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:cifs/target /impersonateuser:admin /ptt
