ABOUT
    Find what processes *modulepart* are loaded into, optionally searching just *procnamepart*

USAGE:
    bof-find-loaded-module --- <modulepart> [opt:procnamepart]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-find-loaded-module --- ntdll

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-find-loaded-module -w 60 --- ntdll
