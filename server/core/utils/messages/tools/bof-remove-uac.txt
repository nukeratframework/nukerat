ABOUT
    Remove UAC flag. Supports standard LDAP (389) with signing/sealing and
    LDAPS (636).
USAGE:
    bof-remove-uac --- {target} {flag} [-ou {path}] [-dc {ip}] [--ldaps]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-remove-uac --- {target} {flag} [-ou {path}] [-dc {ip}] [--ldaps]

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-remove-uac -w 60 --- {target} {flag} [-ou {path}] [-dc {ip}] [--ldaps]
