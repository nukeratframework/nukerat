ABOUT
    List files in a directory. Supports wildcards (e.g. "C:\Windows\S*") unlike the CobaltStrike `ls` command

USAGE:
    bof-dir --- [directory] [subdirs]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-dir --- .
    bof-dir --- . 1

NOTES:
    Use 1 as second argument for recursive listing (not /s).
    Relative paths (e.g. .) work reliably; drive-letter paths may fail depending on argument parsing.

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-dir -w 60 --- C:\Windows
