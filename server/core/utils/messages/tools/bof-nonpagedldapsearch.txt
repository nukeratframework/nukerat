ABOUT
    Execute LDAP searches without paging.

USAGE:
    bof-nonpagedldapsearch --- <query> [--attributes] [--count] [--scope] [--hostname] [--dn] [--ldaps]

EXAMPLES:
    bof-nonpagedldapsearch --- (objectClass=user)
    bof-nonpagedldapsearch --- (objectClass=computer) *,ntsecuritydescriptor

NUKERAT FLAGS:
    -w <seconds>    Maximum wait time until timeout
    -bg             Fork and run without waiting for output

    Example:
    bof-nonpagedldapsearch -w 60 --- (objectClass=user)
